Agriculture

Ericius Farming

Direct cultivation and livestock operations — the raw-produce base the rest of the group's agribusiness arm is built on.

Overview

Ericius Farming runs the group's own crop and livestock operations. It's the starting point of the chain: everything Ericius Produce Trading resells and Ericius Entertainment grills at its chisa nyama grills begins here.

The division is run on the same standard as every other business in the group — planned output, tracked costs, and a clear owner for every plot and herd, rather than informal, undocumented farming.

What we do

  • Seasonal crop cultivation and harvest planning
  • Livestock rearing and animal health management
  • Land and input management across group-owned plots
  • Supplying produce directly into Ericius Produce Trading and Ericius Entertainment

Why it's part of Ericius Group

Farming anchors the whole group's food chain — a division that supplies two others directly is worth running to a higher standard than a typical smallholding, which is exactly the point of pulling it under Ericius Group.
