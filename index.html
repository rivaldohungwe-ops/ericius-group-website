About the Group

Built like a hedgehog defends itself — close, layered, and hard to knock over.

Ericius Group of Companies is a Zimbabwean multi-sector holding company. Rather than betting on one industry, we build a portfolio of independently run businesses that share one back office, one standard of discipline, and one name.

Each division is led by its own team and judged on its own numbers, but every business in the group reports into the same finance and governance structure — the same way a hedgehog's quills are separate, but they all belong to one animal.
